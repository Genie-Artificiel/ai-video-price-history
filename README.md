# AI video, avatar and voice tool pricing history (2024-2026)

What the cheapest paid plans of 15 AI video, avatar and voice tools cost, and how many seconds or minutes of
output they buy each month, from 2024-05-20 to 2026-10-16. Each row is one plan on one date, read from the
vendor's official pricing page as archived by the [Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org/), or from the live
page on the date it was checked (2026-10-05, or 2026-10-16 for Kling 4.0).

Analysis, charts and method: [AI Video Pricing Since 2024: Same Plan, Less Video](https://www.genie-artificiel.com/en/ai-data/ai-video-price-history/)
([French version](https://www.genie-artificiel.com/fr/donnees-ia/evolution-prix-video-ia/)).

Tools covered: Runway, Kling AI, Luma Dream Machine, Hailuo AI (MiniMax), Pika, Vidu and PixVerse (video
generators); Synthesia, HeyGen, Colossyan and D-ID (avatar video); ElevenLabs, Murf and Descript (voice);
InVideo AI (script-to-video).

as decimal separator, without currency sign (`8.8` = $8.80). An empty cell means the figure was not displayed
or not recorded.

are kept so that both copies can be compared and cited under the same name.

## Quick start

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv("data/historique-prix-outils-ia.csv", encoding="utf-8-sig")

# Implied cost per second of video on each entry plan (a calculation, not a published rate)
video = df[(df["per_month_unit"] == "s de vidéo/mois") & (df["currency"] == "USD")].copy()
video["usd_per_second"] = video["monthly_price_billed_monthly"] / video["video_or_voice_per_month"]
print(video[["tool", "date", "newest_model", "usd_per_second", "confidence"]])
```

## Columns

Full definitions, in English and French, are in `data/historique-prix-outils-ia-dictionary.csv`.

| Column | What it contains |

| `date` | Date of the source (YYYY-MM-DD): Wayback Machine capture date, or check date for a live official page |
| `plan` | Plan name as displayed by the vendor |
| `monthly_price_billed_monthly`, `monthly_price_billed_annually` | Price per month on monthly and on annual billing, in `currency`, as displayed (before tax unless the page says otherwise) |
| `currency` | `USD`, or `EUR` for the InVideo AI row of 2026-10-05 (page viewed from France); prices are not converted |
| `included`, `included_unit` | Quantity included in the plan, as displayed (credits, minutes, hours…), and its unit |
| `newest_model`, `resolution` | Newest model the vendor promoted on that date, and the resolution used to convert the plan into a duration |
| `video_or_voice_per_month`, `per_month_unit` | What the plan buys per month with that model, calculated from the vendor's figures; empty when the plan cannot be converted into a duration (unlimited plan, rate not displayed or not found) |

| `source` | URL of the source (Wayback Machine capture or live official page), sometimes followed by a note in parentheses; the URL is the text before the first space |
| `conversion_source` | Where the credit rate was read: usually a URL followed by a quoted excerpt; empty when the rate is on the `source` page |

## Method

- **Plan:** the cheapest paid plan for an individual, at the month-to-month price; annual pricing has its own
  column.
- **Model:** the newest model the vendor promoted on each date, at standard resolution (720p or 768p) when the
  page states it, without paid options (native audio, 1080p). Exception: Kling 4.0 has no silent mode, so sound
  is counted.
- **Conversion:** credits are converted into seconds or minutes with the vendor's own rate (`credit_conversion`,
  `conversion_source`). When a clip's price is not proportional to its length (Luma Ray3.2: 100 credits for 5
  seconds, 300 for 10), the shortest length is used.
- **Dates:** the Wayback Machine captures closest to June and December 2024, June and December 2025, and April
  2026 (within six weeks), plus the captures around each change; then the live page on 2026-10-05 (2026-10-16
  for Kling 4.0).
- **Currency:** U.S. dollars as displayed, before tax. One row (InVideo AI, 2026-10-05) was displayed in euros
  because the page was viewed from France; it is kept in euros, and the page does not say whether VAT is
  included.

## Confidence levels

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `high` | Price, quota and credit rate published by the vendor for that date (pricing page or its capture, release notes, help center or user guide) |
| `medium` | Derived or estimated, taken from another date, read in an archived API response or script rather than on the displayed page, or shown in the generator without being published |
| `low` | Substitute or incomplete source |

Examples of `medium`: Luma's August 2024 clip length, inferred from its product page; the Gen-3 Alpha rate, read
from a February 2025 capture; Kling 4.0's cost (2026-10-16), shown in Kling's generator but not published on its
pricing page or in its official guide.

## Limits

- Costs per second are implied subscription rates, calculated from the vendors' figures. They are not API
  prices, and they do not measure video quality or how many attempts a usable shot takes.
- One plan per tool, the entry-level one: this is not a full price list.
- Gaps: no usable archived pricing page for Vidu in 2024 (prices read from the archived API behind its pricing
  page, from May 2025); no archived PixVerse plan price before late September 2026; no Kling 2.5 or 2.6 credit
  rates in an archived official source.
- Hailuo labels its prices as promotional throughout the period. Through April 2026, the price used is the one
  in the page's FAQ, the only part readable in archived captures.
- Some vendors show different prices depending on the visitor's country (Synthesia, for example). Each row
  records what the cited capture or page displayed.
- Older, cheaper models often remain available: on 2026-10-05, Runway Standard buys 52 s of Gen-4.5 or 104 s of
  Gen-4 Turbo. The `newest_model` cell then names the cheapest model in parentheses (`le moins cher : …`).

## Example

Runway Standard costs $15 a month for 625 credits in every row. With the newest model of each date, it bought
125 s of Gen-2 video on 2024-05-26, 52 s of Gen-4 on 2025-05-31, 25 s of Gen-4.5 on 2026-01-07, then 52 s again
after Gen-4.5's price cut (2026-06-29 capture). Each of these rows links to its Wayback Machine capture in the
`source` column.
